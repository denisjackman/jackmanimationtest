# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-26_

This privacy policy covers the personal WhatsApp assistant ("the assistant") operated by Denis Jackman
under the Jackmanimation WhatsApp Business Account. It is a personal, non-commercial project, not a
product or service offered to the public.

## What data is processed

When you message the assistant on WhatsApp:

- Your message text is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate a reply.
- Your WhatsApp phone number is used to keep track of the conversation so the assistant has context
  from your recent messages.
- The assistant's replies are sent back to you via the WhatsApp Business Platform (operated by Meta).

## What data is stored

A short rolling history (the most recent messages) of your conversation with the assistant is stored
on private infrastructure operated by Denis Jackman, for the sole purpose of maintaining conversation
context. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising.

## Third parties involved

- **Anthropic** processes message text to generate responses, under Anthropic's own API terms.
- **Meta** operates the WhatsApp Business Platform used to send and receive messages.

## Your choices

You can stop using the assistant at any time by simply not messaging it. To request deletion of your
stored conversation history, contact denis.jackman@gmail.com.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to denis.jackman@gmail.com.
