*Last updated: 21 October 2026*

Jackmanimation ("we", "us") runs the website at https://denisjackman.github.io/jackmanimationtest/ (the "Site"). This statement explains what personal data we collect through the Site, why we collect it, and what rights you have under the UK and EU General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR and EU GDPR).

**Who is responsible for your data:** Jackmanimation, contactable at jackmanimationgamingstudio@gmail.com.

## 1. What data we collect

- **Contact details.** If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write in the message.


## 2. How we use your data and our legal basis

| Purpose | Data used | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Replying to your messages | Contact details and message content | Legitimate interests (answering you) |
| Understanding how the Site is used | Analytics data | Consent for non-essential cookies; otherwise legitimate interests |
| Running and securing the Site | Technical data from your visit | Legitimate interests |
| Meeting legal obligations | Any data we are required to keep | Legal obligation |

## 3. Who we share your data with

We don't sell your personal data. We share it only with the service providers that help us run the Site:

- **GitHub** (GitHub Pages), which hosts the Site and handles visitor requests.
- **Google Mail**, which handles the emails sent to our contact address.

These providers may only use your data to provide their services to us.

## 4. International transfers

Some of our service providers are based outside the UK or EEA, including in the United States. Where this happens, we rely on safeguards the law recognises, such as the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses. 

## 5. How long we keep your data

- Emails are kept only as long as needed to reply to you and deal with the matter, then deleted.
- Analytics data is kept according to the retention settings of the analytics provider. 

## 6. Your rights

You have the right to:

- access the personal data we hold about you;
- ask us to correct data that is wrong;
- ask us to delete your data;
- ask us to restrict or object to how we use it;
- ask for your data in a portable format;
- withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent.

To use any of these rights, email jackmanimationgamingstudio@gmail.com. We will respond within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) in the UK at [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk), or to the data protection authority in your EU country.

## 7. Children

The Site isn't aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them. If you think a child has sent us their data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your data. The Site is served over HTTPS.

## 9. Links to other sites

The Site may link to other websites. This statement doesn't cover those sites. Check their privacy statements before giving them any personal data.

## 10. Changes to this statement

We may update this statement from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed.
